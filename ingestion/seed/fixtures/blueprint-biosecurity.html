Nonprofit / Pandemic Preparedness

Strengthening Pandemic Preparedness Through Pathogen-Agnostic Countermeasures

Blueprint Biosecurity is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit advancing practical, pathogen-agnostic countermeasures to reduce the impact of future biological threats.

View Programs
View Latest Updates

Blueprint Biosecurity prioritizes work that is evidence-driven, scalable, and relevant to decision-makers navigating pandemic preparedness, prevention and response.

Our Programs

Our programs focus on countermeasures that have the potential to meaningfully strengthen pandemic preparedness and response across a range of biological threats.

  • PPE

    Respiratory Protection

    Strengthening reusable respirator stockpiles, addressing barriers to effective use by critical workers, and assessing surge manufacturing strategies during emergencies.

    Learn More
  • Built Environment

    Glycol Vapors

    Assessing an underexplored air treatment approach that has the potential to mitigate pathogens in occupied indoor spaces.

    Learn More
  • Built Environment

    Far-UVC

    Assessing the potential of far-UVC light as a safe, effective, continuous disinfection method in occupied spaces.

    Learn More

Supporting the Development of High-Impact Countermeasures

Blueprint Biosecurity supports targeted research and strategic projects that address critical gaps in pandemic preparedness and response. This includes advancing evidence, clarifying implementation pathways, and helping promising approaches move closer to broader real-world use.
